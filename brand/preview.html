05 / Social & Guardrails

Open Graph Card (1200×630) & Usage Rules

social/og-card.svg pairs the studio manifesto and discipline badges with a sculptural 2×2 Cornerstone Matrix card.

✓ Do — Craft & Restraint

  • Reserve Hanko Vermilion (#d9381e / #ff6b4a) for the brand stone, primary play action, and decisive board markers (< 10% of viewport).
  • Maintain at least one 24u cell (37.5% of mark height) of clearspace around all marks and lockups.
  • Pair tabular-nums monospace for clocks, coordinates, and payoff matrices so numbers never jitter mid-match.
  • Load fonts exclusively from system stacks or same-origin .woff2 files—never external ad/tracking CDNs.

✕ Don’t — Visual or Ethical Noise

  • Never pulse, bounce, or repurpose the Vermilion Stone as an unread notification badge or FOMO countdown dot.
  • Never distort the 2×2 grid aspect ratio, swap quadrant positions, or apply neon drop-shadows and gradients.
  • Never place dark-ink marks over dark slate surfaces without inheriting --qa-ink (#f0eee9) via currentColor.
  • Never pair the Quiet Arcade seal with streak-loss penalties, interstitial ads, or dark-pattern modals.
